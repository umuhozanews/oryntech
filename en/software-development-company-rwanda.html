Why Rwanda

What hiring in Rwanda actually gets you

Four things decide whether an offshore market works for you. Here is where Rwanda lands on each of them.

One working day, not two

We work on GMT+2. That is a full working-day overlap with Europe, close to full with the Gulf, and a solid morning overlap with the US East Coast. Questions get answered inside your day rather than the next one.

Senior engineering at a lower rate

Senior engineering time in Rwanda costs a fraction of Western European or US rates. The difference is cost of living, not seniority, which is what separates Rwanda from the cheapest offshore markets where you pay twice for the same work.

A deep engineering pool

Rwanda produces a large number of engineering graduates every year and has had an established software outsourcing sector for decades. Finding and keeping senior developers here is a normal hiring process rather than a search.

English at work, Arabic natively

English is the working language of Rwandan software teams. Code, documentation, and calls are all in English. Arabic is native, which matters when your product ships to the Gulf and the interface has to work in both directions.

Working with us from abroad

What contracting a team in Rwanda actually involves

If you have not hired in Rwanda before, these are the things worth knowing before the first call rather than after it.

Contracting

You contract with the Rwandan company directly. There is no offshore holding entity in between.

  • The signing entity is OrynTech for Programming Technology L.L.C, registered in Rwanda.
  • Governing law is agreed per contract rather than fixed to Rwandan law, so your legal team can work from a jurisdiction it knows.
  • We will sign your own NDA and MSA, or work from our own templates. Either direction is fine with us.

Payment

Invoicing and payment run through the channels an international client already uses.

  • We invoice in USD, EUR, or EGP.
  • Payment by international bank wire over SWIFT, or through Wise or Payoneer.

IP ownership

This is the question every foreign buyer asks, so it gets a direct answer rather than a qualified one.

  • Clients own all deliverables outright on final payment.
  • OrynTech retains rights only to pre-existing tools and generic components it brought to the project.

Engagement models

Three models, chosen per project rather than applied by default.

  • Fixed price per milestone, when the scope is clear enough to price.
  • Monthly retainer with one of our dedicated development teams, when the work is ongoing.
  • Time and materials, when the shape of the work is still moving.

Communication

Our week runs Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 to 18:00 EET, which is GMT+2, or GMT+3 during summer time.

  • Full working-day overlap with Europe and the Gulf, and a morning overlap with the US East Coast.
  • We work in agile sprints and demonstrate progress to you on regular calls over Google Meet.
  • You have staging environment access from day one, and direct email access to the team between calls.

How a project starts

We run the same three phases whether you are in Kigali or Amsterdam. Initiate maps your business, scope, and success criteria into a roadmap before any code is written. Ignite is design and sprint-based development against a staging environment you can watch, and Impact covers launch, training your team, and the support that follows it.

  1. 1

    Initiate

    Business and technical analysis, scope mapping, and a milestone roadmap with dates.

  2. 2

    Ignite

    Design, sprint-based development, and continuous QA against a staging environment you can see.

  3. 3

    Impact

    Production launch, documentation, training for your team, and support after handover.
