Property Hub: Cross-Platform Property Discovery App

A React Native property listing app, released on the App Store and Google Play, that helps users search, compare, and save properties through lists and an interactive map.

ServicesMobile Apps
Property Hub product preview

Overview

Property Hub is a cross-platform mobile app that helps people discover, compare, and save property listings. Users start with featured properties, search by keyword, narrow results with filters for price, type, and rating, and sort by price, rating, or proximity. An interactive map lets them search near their current location, and every property has a detail page with a gallery, amenities, a location map, and a direct way to contact the owner. Shared links open a specific property or a user’s favorites directly, and notifications bring users back when new listings, price changes, or promotions appear. The app is live on both the App Store and Google Play.

The Challenge

How do you help people find the right property quickly on a phone, without losing them in long lists?

Layer the discovery: featured listings, keyword search, filters, sorting, and map search. Keep each property’s details, favorites, and deep links one tap away.

Our Approach

One React Native app, released on both the App Store and Google Play.

01 / WORKSTREAM

Authentication & Onboarding

Onboarding screens that appear once, with registration, login, and OTP-based password reset.

02 / WORKSTREAM

Discovery: Search, Filters & Map

Featured listings, keyword search, filters for price, type, and rating, sorting, and map search near the user’s location.

03 / WORKSTREAM

Property Details, Contact & Favorites

Detail pages with a gallery and map, a contact-the-owner flow, and a saved favorites list.

04 / WORKSTREAM

Notifications & Deep Linking

Alerts for new listings, price changes, and promotions, plus links that open a property or the favorites list directly.
