Confidentiality

How white-label stays white-label

The rules we follow on every agency project.

  • NDA before kickoff

    We sign your NDA, or ours, before you share the brief or any client details. Confidentiality continues after the project ends.

  • Your brand, your client

    We work under your agency's name. When your client needs to talk to the team, we join as part of your agency. Nothing we deliver carries the OrynTech name unless you want it to.

  • All code and IP belong to you

    In most projects, the source code, designs and documentation become yours on final payment, ready to pass to your client. The exact terms are set in our contract with you.

  • Support after launch, still under your brand

    Fixes, updates and new features after go-live, handled by the same team and still delivered in your name.

White label web development means an agency sells and manages a website or web application, while a partner like OrynTech builds it behind the scenes. The client deals only with the agency and sees only the agency's brand. For the agency, it is a way to take on more projects, or new kinds of work, without hiring and managing a full development team. For us, it means working under your name, following your process, and speaking to your client, when needed, as part of your agency. We sign an NDA before kickoff, and code and IP usually transfer to you on final payment. Agencies use it for single projects, for overflow work that comes and goes, or for a dedicated team that works inside the agency for as long as it is needed.
