Our Process

Five steps, one accountable partner

A predictable rhythm that scales from a two-week sprint to a two-year platform build.

01

Discovery

We listen. Workshops, audits, and stakeholder mapping to surface the real problem before we reach for a solution.

02

Strategy

Goals, KPIs, scope, and risk. A written plan you can take to your board and to your engineers.

03

Design

Research-led UX, Creiden-crafted UI, and accessible-by-default patterns. Prototyped, tested, refined.

04

Development

Two-week sprints, weekly demos, one accountable tech lead. We scope for an early MVP so the first real launch is smooth, not rushed.

05

Launch & Support

We don't disappear at go-live. SLAs, monitoring, and iteration for as long as you need us.
